seori-uploader Privacy Policy

seori-uploader is used only by the owner of @seori.gram.

It accesses only the owner's own TikTok account through the official TikTok API, solely to upload videos the owner created.

No personal data of other users is collected, stored, sold or shared.

Access tokens are stored privately and can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings.
